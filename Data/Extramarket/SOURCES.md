# Sources for macro-economic data

## [US Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/)

- electricity_kwh: Electricity prices in USD per kw/h
- gasoline_gallon: Gasoline prices in USD per gallon
- household-gas_therm: Household gas prices in USD per therm
- housing: Average housing prices in USD 
- inflation_cpi: Inflation consumer price index

## [Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/)

- fx_JPUS, fx_USEU, fx_USUK: Foreign exchange rates for respectively japanese yen, euro, and british pound
- interest_rates: Interest rates for Bank Loan Premiums (BLP) and Federal Fuds (FF)
- treasury_yield: US bills/bonds yield rates over 3 months (3mo) and 10 years (10y)
- US_GDP: US Gross Domestic Product

## [Yahoo! finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/) (fetched with yfinance API)

- VIX: Volatility IndeX
